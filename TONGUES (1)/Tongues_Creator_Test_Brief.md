# TONGUES x Creator - Paid Test Collab Brief

## The deal
One short vertical video (Reel/TikTok, 20-45s), flat rate agreed up front, posted on your account. If it performs, we talk about a bigger ongoing partnership. We pay on posting, not on performance.

## What the video is
You, in your usual style, using Tongues to learn exactly the thing YOU choose - any language, any dialect, any topic. The whole point of the app is that you pick: Cairo street slang, Norwegian football chants, the words a textbook would never teach you. The more specific and personal your pick, the better the video.

## Non-negotiables
1. The hook is the deliverable. Film 3 alternate hooks (first 3 seconds) against the same body footage. Name something impossibly specific in the hook - that specificity IS the product demo.
2. Show the choosing moment: on screen, pick your language/dialect/topic in the app.
3. Sound on, captions on, your normal editing style. Do not make it look like an ad.
4. No em dashes or corporate voice in captions.

## Two-cut structure
Cut 1 (organic): your spiciest version - slang, swear words, unhinged specificity welcome.
Cut 2 (clean): same video, ad-safe language, for us to run as paid.
Usage: we get paid-ads usage rights to both cuts for 90 days; you keep posting rights forever.
